## Overview

Adobe Captivate is an eLearning authoring tool that enables users to create various kinds of interactive and fully responsive e-learning content, including software demonstrations, branching scenarios, virtual tours, simulations, and more.

## Features

- Advanced Actions
- Fluid Boxes
- Text To Speech
- HTML5 Publishing
- Branching Scenarios
- Closed Captioning
- VR Capabilities
- Themes And Templates
