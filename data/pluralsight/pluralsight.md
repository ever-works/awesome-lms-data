## Overview

Pluralsight is a learning management system and technology skills platform designed for aspiring and established technology professionals.

## Features

- Comprehensive course library covering software development, IT operations, data, security, and cloud
- Skill assessments and role IQ measurements
- Expert instructors
- Offline access to course content
- Learning paths and skill development plans
- Enterprise analytics and team management

## Pricing

Subscription-based pricing for individuals and enterprises. Plans vary by features and team size.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).
