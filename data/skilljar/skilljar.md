## Overview

Skilljar is a customer education LMS that transforms learning into an engaging, personalized experience. With seamless integrations and flexible monetization options, Skilljar accelerates onboarding and boosts retention, ensuring customers see product value quickly.

## Features

- Seamless integrations
- Flexible monetization options
- Customer onboarding
- Personalized learning experiences

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)

## Pricing Model

Paid — starting price available upon request; no free plan publicly listed.

## Target Audience

Startups, SMBs, Mid-Market, Enterprises
