## Overview

Udemy is an online learning platform that enables instructors to create and sell courses on a wide range of topics, while learners can purchase courses for lifetime access.

## Features

- Over 210,000 courses across business, technology, design, and personal development
- Courses taught by independent instructors
- Lifetime access to purchased courses
- Mobile and TV apps for learning on the go
- Certificate of completion for paid courses
- Free courses available

## Pricing

Courses are priced individually, typically ranging from free to several hundred dollars. Udemy frequently offers discounts. Instructors receive a revenue share based on how the student was acquired.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).
