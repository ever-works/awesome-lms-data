## Overview

Murf AI is a DIY AI voice-over tool that converts scripts or home-style voice recordings into studio-quality audio for videos, presentations, or text-to-speech requirements.

## Features

- Voice Modulation
- User Interface
- API Integration
- Voice Editing
- Voice Cloning
- Voice Customization
- Voice Over Integration
- Voice Effects

## Pricing

- Free: $0 per month
- Creator: $29 per month
- Business: $99 per month

## Target Segments

Startups, SMBs, Mid-Market, Enterprises
