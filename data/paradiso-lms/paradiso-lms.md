## Overview

Paradiso LMS is an enterprise-grade corporate LMS designed to streamline workforce training, compliance certification, and leadership development across global departments and business units.

## Key Features

- Automated Enrollments
- Multi-Tenant Architecture
- Advanced Analytics Dashboards
- Multilingual Support
- Customizable Interface
- Gamification
- SCORM Compliance
- Content Library
- Mobile Learning
- E-Commerce Integration

## Pricing Model

Paid: $5 per user/month (USA pricing)

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)
