## Overview

Abara LMS is a modern, mobile-first Learning Management System built for corporates, training providers, and eLearning companies. It is designed to serve small to medium businesses as well as large enterprises.

## Features

- Gamification
- API Integration
- Discussion Forums
- Blended Learning
- SCORM Compliance
- E Commerce
- Reporting And Analytics
- Course Management

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Deployment

Mobile-first platform suitable for corporate and eLearning environments.
