## Overview

EDUMAAT is an enhanced ERP that automates the education sector, starting from student admission to graduation. An educational institute is a place where the management, the students, the parents, and the faculty are identified as stakeholders.

## Deployment & Pricing

- **Deployment:** Cloud
- **Pricing:** Free plan available. EDUMAAT Basic in Cloud and EDUMAAT Advanced in Cloud are both listed at $0 per student per month.

## Features

- Communication Tools
- Admissions Management
- Teacher Portal
- Attendance Management
- Event Management
- Transport Management
- Hostel Management
- Gradebook

## User Sentiment

Customers praise EDUMAAT for its user-friendly interface, wide feature set, strong customization, modular design, and responsive customer support that speeds implementation and automation. Recurring negatives include IP/session errors, occasional QR/barcode challenges, and some difficulty with bulk Excel uploads.
