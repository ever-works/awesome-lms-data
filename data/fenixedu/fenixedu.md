## Overview

FenixEdu is an open-source academic management system (AMS) and learning management system developed collaboratively by Portuguese universities, including Instituto Superior Técnico and Universidade de Aveiro.

## Features

- Open-source under the Apache License 2.0
- Student information system (SIS) capabilities
- Course and curriculum management
- Enrollment and registration
- Grade management
- Academic calendar
- Self-hosted deployment
- Modular architecture
- Multi-institution support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under Apache License 2.0. Self-hosted deployment with no licensing fees.
