## Overview

Udacity is an online learning platform focused on career-relevant technical education through Nanodegree programs and individual courses.

## Features

- Nanodegree programs in deep learning, machine learning, web development, data science, and more
- Industry-relevant curriculum developed with company partners
- Hands-on projects
- Mentorship and support
- Flexible, self-paced learning
- Career services and resume reviews

## Pricing

Nanodegree programs are paid subscriptions. Some free courses are available.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).
