## Overview

SWAD (Secreto Web para el Aprendizaje de Documentos) is an open-source learning management system developed at the University of Granada in Spain for university-level teaching and learning.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- Course and subject management
- Document and file sharing
- Communication tools (forums, announcements)
- Assessment and grade management
- Student tracking
- Self-hosted deployment
- Spanish and multilingual support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPL license. Self-hosted with no licensing fees.
