## Overview

aTutor is an open-source web-based learning management system (LMS) used to develop and deliver online courses.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- SCORM 1.2 and SCORM 2004 compliant
- Accessibility-focused design (WCAG compliant)
- Self-hosted deployment
- Content authoring tools
- User management and course administration
- Themes and modules support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPL license. Self-hosted with no licensing fees.
