## Overview

Khan Academy is a non-profit educational organization providing free online learning tools for students, teachers, and parents.

## Features

- Free access to all content
- Wide range of subjects including math, science, economics, history, and more
- Video lessons and practice exercises
- Personalized learning dashboard
- Materials for instructors and coaches
- Test preparation (SAT, LSAT, MCAT, etc.)

## Pricing

Completely free; supported by donations.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).
