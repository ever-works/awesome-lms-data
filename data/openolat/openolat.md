## Overview

OpenOLAT is an open-source web-based learning management system (LMS) developed primarily by the University of Zurich. It is used by educational institutions and enterprises for online courses, assessments, and collaboration.

## Features

- Open-source under the Apache License 2.0
- Course authoring and management
- Assessment and testing tools
- SCORM-compliant content import/export
- Collaborative tools (forums, wikis, groups)
- User and role management
- Learning paths and curricula
- Calendar and appointment scheduling
- Mobile-responsive interface
- Self-hosted deployment
- Multi-language support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under Apache License 2.0. Self-hosted deployment with no licensing fees.
