## Overview

e-khool LMS is an AI-powered learning management system designed for corporate training, education, and skill development. It offers a comprehensive platform for course delivery, learner management, and analytics.

## Features

- AI-powered learning recommendations
- Course authoring and management
- Assessments and certifications
- Gamification and badges
- Mobile learning app
- Analytics and reporting
- SCORM and xAPI support
- Integration with HRIS and third-party tools
- White-label customization
- Cloud-hosted deployment
