## Overview

BizLibrary is a corporate learning and development platform designed to help organizations build a culture of continuous learning and development. It provides award-winning solutions to inspire employees, enhance skills, and drive engagement.

## Features

- Integration Capabilities
- Reporting And Analytics
- Performance Management
- Content Library
- Microlearning
- Learning Paths
- Learning Plans
- Custom Branding

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)

## Pricing Model

Paid — starting price available upon request; no free plan publicly listed.

## Target Audience

Startups, SMBs, Mid-Market, Enterprises
