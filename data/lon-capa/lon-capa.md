## Overview

LON-CAPA (Learning Online Network with Computer-Assisted Personalized Approach) is an open-source course management system and content sharing network primarily used in higher education STEM disciplines.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- Course management and delivery
- Personalized assessment with algorithmically generated questions
- Content sharing across institutions
- Dropbox for assignments
- Gradebook and student tracking
- Discussion forums
- Self-hosted deployment
- Supports math and science content

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPL license. Self-hosted with no licensing fees.
