## Overview

OpenLearning is an online course platform and learning management system (LMS) that enables educators, institutions, and organizations to create and deliver online courses with a focus on social and community-driven learning.

## Features

- Course creation and authoring tools
- Social learning activities and community features
- Student progress tracking
- Certificates and badges
- Mobile app support
- White-label options for institutions
- Payment integration for selling courses
- Analytics and insights
- Cloud-hosted deployment
- Integrations with third-party tools

## Pricing

Freemium model. Free plan available for individual educators. Paid plans for institutions and businesses with advanced features and white-labeling.
