## Overview

EthosCE is a learning management system (LMS) built for continuing education providers, professional associations, and academic institutions. It supports course delivery, accreditation management, and e-commerce for selling educational content.

## Features

- Course catalog and learning paths
- Accreditation and credit tracking
- E-commerce for course sales
- Registration and enrollment management
- Reporting and analytics
- Mobile-responsive design
- SCORM and AICC support
- Integration with association management systems
- Certificate generation
- Cloud-hosted deployment
