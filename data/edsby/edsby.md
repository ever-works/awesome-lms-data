## Overview

Edsby is a cloud-based learning management system (LMS) and data platform purpose-built for K-12 school districts. It connects teachers, students, parents, and administrators in a unified system.

## Features

- Gradebook and assessment management
- Attendance tracking
- Parent portal and engagement tools
- Student information system (SIS) integration
- Course and class management
- Persistent groups and communities
- Analytics and reporting for administrators
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Timetable and scheduling integration
- Cloud-hosted deployment

## Pricing

Commercial/paid product. Pricing is based on district size and student count. Contact vendor for quotes.
