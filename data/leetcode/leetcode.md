## Overview

LeetCode is an online platform for practicing coding problems and preparing for technical interviews.

## Features

- Comprehensive problem library across algorithms, data structures, and databases
- Company-specific interview questions
- Active community and discussion forums
- Mock interviews and contests
- Skill progression tracking

## Pricing

Free tier with basic problem access. Premium subscription unlocks additional problems, company tags, and interview simulations.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).
