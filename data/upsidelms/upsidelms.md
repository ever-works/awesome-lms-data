## Overview

UpsideLMS is an AI-powered, cloud-based LMS serving over 3 million users worldwide, built for enterprises to manage workforce training and development.

## Key Features

- User Management
- Blended Learning
- Gamification
- Assessment Engine
- Reporting and Analytics
- Virtual Classroom
- Certification Management
- Multi-Tenant Architecture
- ILT (Instructor-Led Training)
- Mobile Learning
