## Overview

Exercism is a free, open-source platform for practicing programming skills through coding exercises and mentorship.

## Features

- Practice problems in over 30 programming languages
- Mentorship from experienced developers
- Wide variety of languages
- Structured learning tracks
- Free of cost
- Open source codebase

## Pricing

Completely free.
