## Overview

Lessonly is learning and training management software designed for capturing and sharing team knowledge, improving skills with feedback, and driving greater productivity. It serves startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprises.

## Features

- Single Sign On (SSO)
- Learning Paths
- Surveys & Feedback
- Knowledge Base
- Reporting And Analytics
- Role Based Permissions
- SCORM Compliance
- API Access
