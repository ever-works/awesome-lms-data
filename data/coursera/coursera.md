## Overview

Coursera is an innovative online learning platform offering diverse courses from top universities. It provides flexible schedules, expert instructors, and certificates upon completion, empowering users to advance their careers and gain new skills at their own pace.

## Pricing Model

Freemium/paid with financial aid available.

## Features

- Verified Certificates
- Specializations
- Wide Range Of Courses
- Flexible Learning
- Financial Aid
- Discussion Forums
- Course Recommendations
- Interactive Quizzes

## Audience

Tailored for StartUps, SMBs, Mid-Market, and Enterprises.

## User Sentiment

Users describe Coursera as one of the best platforms for youth and professionals who want to gain skills, supporting courses from top universities worldwide with certifications that carry market value. Some reviews note value-for-money concerns.
