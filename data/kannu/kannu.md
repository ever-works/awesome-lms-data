## Overview

Kannu is a cloud-based learning management system (LMS) developed by Kadenze, Inc. It enables institutions and educators to create and deliver online courses with a focus on media-rich and creative education.

## Features

- Course creation and management
- Media-rich content support (video, audio, images)
- Student enrollment and management
- Assignment and grading tools
- Discussion forums
- Mobile-responsive interface
- Analytics and insights
- Cloud-hosted deployment
- Branding and customization options
