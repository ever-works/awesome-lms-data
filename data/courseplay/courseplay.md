## Overview

Courseplay is a next-generation learning experience platform (LXP) designed for 360-degree employee growth. It combines on-the-job, social, and formal learning in a single workplace platform.

## Features

- User Management
- Mobile Compatibility
- Role Based Access
- Discussion Forums
- SCORM Compliance
- Custom Branding
- Reporting And Analytics
- Course Creation

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises
