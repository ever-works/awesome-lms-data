## Overview

Codecademy is an interactive online learning platform focused on teaching programming and technical skills through a learn-by-doing approach.

## Features

- Interactive coding lessons in the browser
- Courses in web development, data science, computer science, and IT
- Beginner-friendly learning paths
- Skill paths and career paths
- Projects and quizzes
- Certificates of completion

## Pricing

Free tier includes basic courses. Pro subscription unlocks advanced content, projects, certificates, and career paths.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).
