## Overview

LearnUpon is a versatile LMS designed to streamline employee onboarding and training. It centralizes learning management, leverages AI for personalized experiences, and supports diverse audiences, driving performance, retention, and growth for businesses.

## Target Audience

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Features

- Custom Branding
- Course Management
- Multilingual Support
- Gamification
- Scorm Compliance
- Content Library
- Learning Paths
- Automated Notifications
