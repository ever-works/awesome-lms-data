## Overview

LAMS (Learning Activity Management System) is an open-source learning design tool that enables educators to create, manage, and deliver sequences of collaborative online learning activities.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- Visual learning design interface
- Activity sequencing and branching
- Collaborative learning tools
- Content repository
- Learner tracking and monitoring
- Self-hosted deployment
- Integration with other LMS platforms via LTI
- Multilingual support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPL license. Self-hosted with no licensing fees.
