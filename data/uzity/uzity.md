## Overview

Uzity is a cloud-based learning management system (LMS) designed for educational institutions and businesses to manage online courses and training programs.

## Features

- Course creation and management
- Student enrollment and management
- Assessment and quiz tools
- Gradebook and reporting
- Discussion forums
- Mobile learning support
- Content management
- User roles and permissions
- Cloud-hosted deployment
- API access
