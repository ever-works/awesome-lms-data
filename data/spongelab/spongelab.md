## Overview

Spongelab is a game-based learning platform and learning management system focused on science education for K-12 and higher education. It combines interactive educational games, simulations, and content with LMS functionality.

## Features

- Game-based learning content (science focus)
- Interactive simulations and videos
- Class and student management
- Assessment and progress tracking
- Content authoring tools
- Badges and achievements
- Lesson planning tools
- Cloud-hosted deployment

## Pricing

Freemium model. Free tier available with basic features. Paid plans for advanced features and institutional use.
