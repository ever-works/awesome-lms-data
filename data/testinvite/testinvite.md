## Overview

TestInvite is a secure online assessment platform for creating and administering exams with proctoring and anti-cheating capabilities. It serves businesses of various sizes from startups to enterprises.

## Features

- Language Support
- Candidate Authentication
- Integration With Lms
- Detailed Analytics
- Instant Feedback
- Timed Assessments
- Automated Grading
- Customizable Templates
- Lockdown mode
- Webcam/screen recording
- Live proctoring
- Chat monitoring
