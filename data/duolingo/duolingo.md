## Overview

Duolingo is a language-learning platform that makes learning a new language fun through gamified, bite-sized lessons.

## Features

- Gamified language lessons
- Points, streaks, and leveling system
- Variety of languages
- Free tier with ads
- Super Duolingo paid subscription (ad-free, offline access)
- Mobile and web applications

## Pricing

Free tier available. Super Duolingo subscription removes ads and adds offline access.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS) with mobile and web apps.
