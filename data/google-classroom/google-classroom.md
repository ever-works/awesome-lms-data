## Overview

Google Classroom is a free web service developed by Google for schools that aims to simplify creating, distributing, and grading assignments in a paperless way. It is part of Google Workspace for Education.

## Features

- Free for educational institutions
- Assignment creation and distribution
- Grading and feedback tools
- Integration with Google Workspace (Docs, Drive, Meet, etc.)
- Class stream for announcements
- Student work tracking
- Guardian email summaries
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Originality reports
- Rubrics
- Cloud-hosted deployment

## Pricing

Free for schools using Google Workspace for Education. Paid editions (Education Plus, Teaching and Learning Upgrade) available with additional features.
