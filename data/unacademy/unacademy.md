## Overview

Unacademy is a learning platform providing live classes, recorded lectures, quizzes, and study notes across competitive exam subjects. It is designed for learners ranging from individuals to enterprise customers.

## Features

- Offline Access
- Interactive Quizzes
- Leaderboards
- Multiple Educators
- Structured Courses
- Live Polls
- Interactive Whiteboard
- Customizable Study Plans

## Pricing

- Free: $0 per month
- Plus: $6 per month
- Iconic: $11 per month
