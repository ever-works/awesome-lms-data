## Overview

imc Learning Suite is an enterprise learning platform from imc AG that combines learning management system (LMS), learning experience platform (LXP), and authoring tool capabilities for corporate training and development.

## Features

- Learning management and course delivery
- Learning experience platform (LXP) features
- Content authoring tools
- Social and collaborative learning
- Mobile learning
- Analytics and reporting
- SCORM, xAPI, and AICC support
- Integration with HR systems and third-party tools
- Multi-tenant support
- Cloud or on-premise deployment
- Multilingual support

Commercial/paid enterprise product. Pricing is customized based on organization size and modules. Contact vendor for quotes.
