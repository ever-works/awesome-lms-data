## Overview

Helium is an open-source color-coded student planner for managing classes, homework, grades, and notes, with smart notifications and multi-device synchronization.

## Features

- Color-coded student planner
- Classes, homework, grades, and notes tracking
- Smart notifications
- Multi-device sync
- Self-hosted deployment
- Web-based interface

## Deployment

Self-hosted (Docker)
