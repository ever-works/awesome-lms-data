## Overview

Open edX is an open-source learning management system (LMS) platform originally developed by edX, a nonprofit organization founded by Harvard University and MIT. It powers the edx.org MOOC platform and is used by academic institutions, corporations, and governments worldwide.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3)
- Course authoring with Studio
- Learner portal and course discovery
- XBlock extensibility architecture
- Mobile app support
- Discussion forums and peer assessments
- Certificate generation
- Analytics and insights
- Self-hosted deployment
- Multi-language support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under AGPLv3. Self-hosted deployment with no licensing fees. Managed hosting and support services available from Open edX partners.
