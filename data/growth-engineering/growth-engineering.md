## Overview

Growth Engineering is a UK-based learning technology company that provides learning management systems (LMS) and learning experience platforms (LXP) with a focus on gamification, social learning, and learner engagement.

## Features

- Gamification (badges, points, leaderboards)
- Social learning feeds
- Learning paths and campaigns
- Mobile app (OttoLearn)
- Content authoring tools
- Analytics and reporting
- SCORM and xAPI compliance
- Integration capabilities
- White-label customization
- Cloud-hosted deployment
