## Overview

eFront is an enterprise learning management system (LMS) developed by Epignosis LLC, designed for corporate training and e-learning deployment.

## Features

- Course creation and management
- User and group management
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- E-commerce integration for selling courses
- White-label customization
- SCORM and xAPI (Tin Can) support
- Mobile learning support
- API and integrations with third-party systems

## Pricing

Commercial/paid licensing. Pricing varies based on deployment model (cloud or self-hosted) and number of users. Contact vendor for pricing details.

## Deployment

Available as cloud-hosted SaaS or self-hosted on-premise deployment.
