## Overview

Claroline is an open-source learning management system (LMS) and course management system written in PHP.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- Written in PHP
- Course creation and management
- Collaborative learning tools
- Self-hosted deployment
- Document sharing
- Calendar and agenda management

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPL license. Self-hosted with no licensing fees.
