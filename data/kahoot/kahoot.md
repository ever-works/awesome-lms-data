## Overview

Kahoot! is a game-based learning platform used in educational institutions and corporate settings for creating and playing interactive quizzes, polls, and learning games.

## Features

- Quiz and game creation
- Real-time student response system
- Question banks and templates
- Reports and analytics
- Mobile app
- Team mode
- Kahoot! Academy creator community
- Integration with Microsoft Teams, PowerPoint, and Google Slides
- Cloud-hosted deployment

## Pricing

Freemium model. Free basic plan for teachers and individuals. Paid plans for schools, higher education, and businesses with advanced features.
