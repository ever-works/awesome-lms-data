## Overview

MapleLMS is a modern-day learning management system with out-of-the-box Salesforce integration. This highly scalable and AI-powered LMS delivers live and on-demand continuing learning and is easy to manage for non-technical users.

## Features

- Mobile Learning
- Learning Paths
- E Commerce
- Certification Management
- Automated Notifications
- Content Library
- Blended Learning
- Social Learning
