## Overview

itslearning is a cloud-based learning management system (LMS) used by K-12 schools and higher education institutions worldwide. It provides a platform for teaching, learning, and communication.

## Features

- Course planning and management
- Content creation and resource sharing
- Assignment submission and grading
- Communication and messaging tools
- Calendar and scheduling
- Student portfolios
- Learning objectives and standards alignment
- Mobile apps
- Analytics and reporting
- Cloud-hosted deployment
- LTI integrations
