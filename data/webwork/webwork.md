## Overview

WeBWorK is an open-source online homework delivery system for mathematics and science courses. It allows instructors to create and assign problems, provides students with instant feedback, and automatically grades submissions.

## Features

- Open-source under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- Algorithmically generated problems with randomization
- Instant feedback for students
- Automatic grading
- Gradebook management
- Problem libraries for calculus, algebra, statistics, physics, and more
- Custom problem authoring
- Self-hosted deployment
- Integration with other LMS platforms

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPL license. Self-hosted with no licensing fees.
