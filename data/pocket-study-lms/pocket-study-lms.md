## Overview

Pocket Study is a new age LMS (Learning Management System) with an advanced mobile app and interesting new models for effective teaching-learning activities. It is a centralized tool for sharing content in the most organized way.

## Features

- Single Sign On (SSO)
- Role Based Access
- Content Library
- Discussion Forums
- Mobile Access
- Customizable Interface
- Assessment Tools
- Progress Tracking
