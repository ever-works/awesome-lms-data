## Overview

EduGyaan is ready-to-use online tutoring software designed for coaching institutes, startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprises. It helps tutors take their offline classes online instantly.

## Key Features

- Reporting And Analytics
- Mobile Access
- Gamification
- Multimedia Support
- Student Management
- Interactive Whiteboard
- Homework Management
- Virtual Classroom

## Target Audience

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Pricing Model

Paid (starting price available on request from vendor).

## Deployment

Cloud/SaaS

## User Sentiment

Customers consistently praise EduGyaan for its ease of use, comprehensive features (live classes, recordings, test series, white-labeled apps), responsive customer support, and affordable pricing. Recurring negatives are limited mainly to a missing native iOS app and minor UI/look-and-feel preferences.
