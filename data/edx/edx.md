## Overview

edX is a global MOOC platform created by Harvard and MIT, offering courses, MicroBachelors, MicroMasters, professional certificates, and full online degrees from leading universities and institutions.

## Features

- Courses from Harvard, MIT, Berkeley, and other top institutions
- Verified certificates for a fee
- MicroBachelors and MicroMasters programs
- Full online degrees
- Audit most courses for free
- Open edX open-source platform available for self-hosting

## Pricing

Many courses can be audited free of charge. Verified certificates typically range from $50 to $300. Professional certificates and full degrees cost more.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS); open-source Open edX available for self-hosted deployment.
