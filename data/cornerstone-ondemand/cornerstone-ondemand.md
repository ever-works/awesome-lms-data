## Overview

Cornerstone OnDemand is a cloud-based human capital management (HCM) and learning management system (LMS) platform for enterprises. It combines learning management with talent management, performance, and recruitment capabilities.

## Features

- Learning management and course delivery
- Content management and curation
- Performance management
- Recruitment and onboarding
- HR and workforce analytics
- Compliance training
- Mobile learning
- AI-powered recommendations
- API and integrations
- Enterprise-grade security and scalability
- Cloud-hosted deployment
